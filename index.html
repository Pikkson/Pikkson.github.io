Model and assumptions

For a horizontal arm, the gravitational torque is estimated as:

τ = g × (m_payload × L + m_arm × L / 2)

Here, the payload acts at the arm tip, and a uniform arm's centre of mass is halfway along its length. The maximum payload is calculated from the selected usable motor torque after subtracting the arm's own torque.

Important: This is a static, idealized estimate. It excludes friction, acceleration, impact loads, gearbox losses, compliance, and changes in motor torque with speed and voltage. Stall torque is not a continuous operating rating. Check the motor datasheet and use an appropriate engineering safety margin before building.
